# Clicroot.com - Keyword Universe & Content/IA Architecture

Greenfield SEO + GEO (AI-visibility) strategy for clicroot.com. Markets: Colombia (primary) + Mexico (secondary), Spanish primary / English secondary. Goal: rank for high-intent SEO + GEO terms in CO + MX, and be cited by LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) when buyers ask for the best SEO/AI agency.

Data source: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer + SERP Overview, pulled 2026-06-25, `country=co` and `country=mx`. All volumes/KD/CPC are real Ahrefs figures unless tagged `(inferred)`. CPC in USD. No em dashes used anywhere.

---

## 0. Headline read

- **MX is the bigger raw market, CO is the easier win.** MX head terms are ~2x CO volume (agencia seo 1300 vs 700) but the MX SERP is defended by established DR 40-54 agencies with deep footprints. CO head terms are smaller but the top of the SERP is soft (DR 8-43, thin keyword counts 31-49, no real GEO positioning). Lead with CO commercial + local, ride MX on the GEO/informational angle where nobody is entrenched.
- **GEO is nascent demand, not zero demand.** "que es geo" is 200/KD0 (MX) and 100/KD0 (CO); "generative engine optimization" 200/KD5 (MX); a real "SEO + inteligencia artificial" micro-cluster exists in MX (agentes de seo de inteligencia artificial 90, el seo y la inteligencia artificial 80, seo con inteligencia artificial 30). This is a land-grab: own the category vocabulary now while KD is 0-5.
- **The commercial money sits in the local + consultant terms, not the GEO terms (yet).** consultor seo (CO 350/KD13, MX 500/KD1), consultoria seo (CO 150/KD2, MX 250/KD1), agencia seo bogota (500/KD2), agencia seo medellin (300/KD7) all carry high CPC ($60-150) and low-to-mid KD. These fund the business while the GEO pillar compounds.

### Competitor read (SERP Overview, 2026-06-25)

`agencia seo colombia` (CO): top of SERP is soft. Strongest organic results: loscreativos.co/seo (DR43, 620 traffic, 49 kw), agenciaseocolombia.com.co (DR26, 638 traffic, 47 kw), agenciaseology.com (DR43, 423 traffic). A listicle (seranking.com DR85) and a LinkedIn pulse rank p9-10. Only one result ("Designs and IT") even mentions GEO. Takeaway: a focused, GEO-positioned CO money page with modest DR can break top 5.

`agencia seo mexico` (MX): more defended. seoenmexico.com (DR40, 1002 traffic, 48 kw, 1295 backlinks), agenciaseomexico.mx (DR36, 2063 backlinks), octopus.mx (DR42, 628 traffic), plus listicles codefy.com.mx (DR54) and marketing4ecommerce.mx (DR68). Takeaway: ranking the MX money page head-on is a 12-18 month backlink fight; win MX first via GEO/informational terms and the lower-KD city pages (Monterrey KD0-1), and pursue citations in the "mejores agencias" listicles.
